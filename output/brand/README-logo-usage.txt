FMV InfraSec - Logo usage

COLOR TRANSPARENT
Use on neutral white, light gray, or near-black backgrounds when maximum brand recognition is desired.

WHITE TRANSPARENT
Use on dark backgrounds, photographs, video, and the portfolio dark theme.

GRAPHITE TRANSPARENT
Use on white backgrounds, printed documents, profile PDFs, and the portfolio light theme.

FULL LOGO
Use in documents, covers, social banners, proposals, and wide spaces.

MARK / ISOTYPE
Use for avatars, favicons, profile icons, and compact square spaces.

Never stretch, rotate, add shadows, change proportions, or place over a busy background without sufficient contrast.
